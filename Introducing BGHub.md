# Introducing BGHub

BGHub is a platform for Basis Guangzhou students to communicate, collaborate, share, and provide help for each other in academic resources such as articles, communication journals (CJ), or files (class powerpoints, documents, images, etc).

## 4 Major Sections

BGHub have 4 major section for students to learn:

1. Articles Section
   
   Students can send articles or read other student's articles, feel free to share your ideas and knowledges!

2.  Communication Journal (CJ) Section
   
   Students can note down or edit a subjects CJ, this can help other students to have a quick glance at the CJ's information if they havn't noted it down! I quick resource for a quick check.

3. Chatting Section
   
   A more free section for students to talk and communicate, students can send messages in a public chat room, have fun to talk and share!

4. Resources Section
   
   Cannot find the PPTs or worksheets on teams? Or you want a better resources to study? This section allows student to post resources they think that can help other student to study, which other students can also download the resources to learn.

## Is this secure?

BGHub uses a ranking system to ensure everything posted is appropriate. In which we have a first secure barrier: AI Audit, most of the resource will be check by AI before its publishment, and this is very safe and quick at the same time. Also, in BGHub ranking system, users are divided into LV0-LV6, you can upgrade through publishing  and sharing, and as you upgrades more, you unlock more free chances(easier to post, less restriction on audits) and more privileges to post things more freely! For details of this system, please go to:
[BGHub Ranking System](https://bghub.top/forum/10)

## Is AI audit trustable?

Yes, the AI scans every post, file, and message for inappropriate content (e.g., profanity, bullying, off-topic material) before anything goes live. And every AI decision is recorded and can be reviewed by human moderators if a user appeals. The AI review system is been tested to ensure its reliability in audit.

## What if someone violated?

BGHub also have a reward and punishment system, in which when a student publish a content that passes the audition, this students can earn more credit to get close to the next level. In the other way, students will lose credit when trying to publish inappropriate context, this depends on how severe it is, in some situation, the student's account can be banned for hours.

## Is this website trustable?

Yes, this website run on ssl certificate, and all user data is encrypted. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. BGHub is maintained by a dedicated team of student moderators from Basis Guangzhou to ensure a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment.

What you can expect:

- No ads, no spam – BGHub is purely for academic support and student community.

- Report system – If any inappropriate content slips through, users can flag it for human review.
